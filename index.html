Table seats

The table has 7 seats. You arrange them on the felt in the Play tab, each round, before dealing — tap a seat to sit down, seat a player, or clear it. Your seat stays yours until you move it.
Applying shoe settings starts a fresh shoe but keeps your seat layout. Reset clears everything.
